# Northstar Prints & Branding Studio — Business Visibility Case

**Project type:** Fictional demonstration consulting case, not actual client work.  
**By:** ClearLedger Business Solutions  
**Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Excel

## The problem
A growing Nigerian print and branding business has order, customer, payment and expense records but lacks a reliable, joined-up management view of sales, costs, profit and cash collections.

## What was developed
A five-page Power BI dashboard covering Owner Overview; Sales & Service Performance; Customer Insights; Cash & Receivables; and Expenses & Profitability. The work includes data-quality assessment, preparation, relationship modelling, measures and management interpretation.

## Key reported figures (demonstration dataset)
| Measure | Reported result |
|---|---:|
| Completed-order sales | ₦355.09M |
| Gross profit | ₦136.87M |
| Gross margin | 38.54% |
| Expenses | ₦126.07M |
| Operating contribution (analytical estimate) | ₦10.80M |
| Completed-order collections (provisional) | ₦312.97M |
| Completed-order collection rate (provisional) | 88.14% |
| Outstanding on completed orders (provisional) | ₦42.12M |

## Main business insight
Strong sales and gross margin do not automatically translate into strong remaining contribution. Recorded operating expenses absorb most of the gross profit. Branded Tee Shirts lead revenue; Polo Shirts lead total gross profit; Graphic Design has the highest gross margin percentage at much lower sales volume.

## Important caveats
This is a fictional case. Some records have unmatched customer/service IDs, missing dates, and payment-order exceptions. Reported cash/receivables figures are provisional analytical indicators, not audited receivables. `Operating contribution` is not accounting net profit. The case study is based on the exported report, not an independent engine-level audit of every DAX measure or model relationship.
